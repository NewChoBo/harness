# Governor / Higher Authority

Resource ID: `role/governor`

## Purpose

Decide whether a reviewed material candidate becomes adopted policy/state within delegated authority.

## Required inputs

- frozen effective candidate identity;
- approved goal/scope and consequence analysis;
- Worker self-check and validation evidence;
- fresh Independent Reviewer verdict;
- unresolved blockers and reserved-boundary assessment;
- rollback/falsifier and expected effect when material.

## Responsibilities

- verify the review applies to the current effective candidate;
- approve, reject, narrow, or request revision;
- integrate only after required review/validation gates pass;
- verify the resulting canonical state after integration;
- route adopted changes to post-adoption effect validation.

## Constraints / non-scope

- never substitute producer self-check for independent review;
- do not adopt a material change to the Governor's own responsibilities/authority; route it to a higher authority;
- do not exceed delegated authority or reserved root/high-risk boundaries;
- do not treat merge success as evidence that the design is effective.

## Evidence / completion

A material adoption record identifies candidate/base/provenance, reviewer evidence, decision, integration result, rollback/effect state, and next owner.
