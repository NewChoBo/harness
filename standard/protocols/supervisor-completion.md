# Supervisor Completion Gate

Before closing a material supervisory run, verify:

1. reported worker/reviewer/research status matches observable evidence;
2. ownership is unique and required independent review exists;
3. blockers and dependencies are ordered correctly;
4. completion has the exact evidence required by its state;
5. no material architecture/ownership/policy contradiction is hidden;
6. exceptional high-severity or repeated failures are routed;
7. recursive-improvement candidates and pending effect validation are tracked;
8. the next highest-value action or question is clear.

The Supervisor normally evaluates control evidence rather than rereading the full source tree.
