# Agent Self-Check

Resource ID: `checklist/agent-self-check`

Before reporting a successful work result, verify:

- **Goal** — the routed goal and acceptance criteria are actually satisfied.
- **Freshness** — control/base, candidate, dependencies, and relevant state were rechecked before final persistence.
- **Scope / ownership** — the work stayed inside assigned authority/write scope and did not duplicate or overwrite foreign work.
- **Authority composition** — when profiles/project/task/lane/runtime overlays are effective, higher precedence was applied only inside already-delegated scope; no overlay widened authority or overrode non-overridable/reserved constraints. Cross-boundary requests were blocked or escalated instead of winning by precedence.
- **Execution depth** — when `protocol/adaptive-effort-convergence` applies, the effective depth/profile/override was proportionate to impact/uncertainty/reversal cost and did not silently expand task scope or authority. The shallowest credible profile was preferred and depth was narrowed when evidence reduced risk/uncertainty.
- **Effort truthfulness** — total/stage floor/target/ceiling or ratios were used only when supported by current budget/clock evidence; no elapsed/remaining time was invented and no floor/pass target was used as a busywork quota.
- **Target discipline** — target effort/pass count was treated as a reassessment boundary. Any work beyond target had a specific material continuation reason; remaining runtime capacity or unused budget alone was not treated as a reason to continue.
- **Ceiling discipline** — at/near ceiling, no new optional production/research/review/refactor/polish was started; remaining capacity was reserved for the nearest required validation/safety/checkpoint/handoff boundary. Unfinished required validation was reported truthfully rather than extended indefinitely or called PASS.
- **Marginal value / overwork** — optional next work had a plausible material effect on correctness, validation, uncertainty, safety, decision quality, preservation, or resumability. Repeated wording/polish, unchanged-candidate review, source-count accumulation, speculative adjacent refactors, shrinking pass delta, or work performed mainly because time remained caused stop/narrow/routing rather than continued utilization.
- **Iteration discipline** — repeated passes were finite, consumed residual/delta work, and had a material continuation reason. `min` did not create artificial sub-passes, `target` was not treated as a minimum, and `max` was not treated as a desired count. Early convergence/`NO_MATERIAL_DELTA` was allowed.
- **Convergence** — done criteria, validation, unresolved material findings/uncertainty, expected marginal value of another pass, target-crossing justification, and opportunity cost were checked. Repeated failure/contradictory evidence/expanding scope triggered replan/handoff/escalation rather than an unbounded loop.
- **Unresolved limit exit** — if a budget/ceiling/max-pass boundary was reached with material work unresolved, the run distinguished a normal resumable semantic slice from a repeated/non-converging diagnostic failure. The same approach was not blindly queued again when progress, dependencies, capability, ownership, or guidance showed a deeper problem.
- **Root-cause routing** — persistent unresolved-at-limit behavior was checked proportionally for decomposition, stale/wrong approach, hidden dependency, evidence/research gap, runtime/tool limitation, ownership/authority mismatch, validation/state-model gap, systemic guidance/architecture/topology gap, external limitation, or policy/reserved-decision conflict. Existing Harness classifications were reused rather than inventing taxonomy merely for timeout accounting.
- **Issue / escalation discipline** — one bounded run ending did not automatically create an Issue. Existing Issue/work-item ownership and a stable target+failure/blocker signature were restored first; persistent decision-ready local causes were routed to one existing/new work item, while cross-scope/systemic/authority causes were reported to the Supervisor or applicable higher agent without duplicate per-run comments/issues.
- **Review balance** — when required by the effective depth profile, Critical/Falsification and Preservation/Constructive perspectives were both considered and reconciled through synthesis; preservation analysis identified what must not regress rather than manufacturing praise.
- **Review churn** — a completed dual-lens cycle with no material actionable delta ended first-party review for the unchanged candidate unless a new material question/evidence/candidate change justified another cycle.
- **Independent-review boundary** — any dual-lens self-review was treated only as first-party execution-quality control and was not represented as formal Independent Review.
- **Validation** — required checks actually ran; cancelled, skipped, unavailable, or unverified is not PASS.
- **Evidence** — exact candidate/commit/resource identity and material results are recorded.
- **Outcome receipt truthfulness** — when `protocol/execution-outcome-receipt` is required, the receipt is bound to the exact work/target/control/effective-Harness subject and reflects the final verified terminal state. Unknown/unavailable clock, target/ceiling, pass, residual-work, or validation evidence was not converted into `false`, `0`, invented values, or PASS. Lifecycle state and execution stop reason remain distinct; normal resumable limit exits are not mislabeled diagnostic, and self-authored receipt fields are not represented as independently verified merely because they were persisted.
- **Receipt/noise discipline** — a durable outcome receipt was created only when required for recovery/effect/audit or the selected first-canary proof obligation. After that first proof, unchanged `NO_ACTION` runs did not create repetitive receipt/comment noise. Blocker fingerprints were used for deduplication only and did not create Issue/escalation authority.
- **Consequences** — known material side effects/regressions and rollback/falsifier are not being hidden.
- **Data boundary** — shared/public persistence contains no consumer/project-specific confidential knowledge, personal/sensitive data, credentials/secrets, private identifiers, or private evidence that belongs in a local/private overlay.
- **North Star** — material architecture/governance/workflow choices do not obviously move the system away from the repository's canonical North Star for local convenience or activity volume.
- **Residual work** — unresolved defects, migration, cleanup, documentation, dependency, rollback, or effect-validation obligations were checked rather than silently discarded.
- **Systemic follow-up** — repeated failure, stale/duplicated policy, ownership/validation/research/provider/tool gaps, repeated unresolved-at-limit behavior, non-converging or overlong effort/review behavior, automation-topology inefficiency, policy contradiction, obsolete mechanism, or a materially simpler alternative were considered proportionally.
- **Routing discipline** — any material residual/systemic finding is surfaced for at most one highest-value follow-up; current implementation scope is not expanded merely because the self-check found more work. `NO_ACTION` is valid.
- **Handoff** — durable state is sufficient for the next owner or later run, including effective depth/override and pass/convergence/stop state when decision-relevant, unresolved-at-limit diagnosis and recommended owner when applicable, exact outcome-receipt identity/reference when required, the selected follow-up/effect obligation, or explicit `NO_ACTION` when material.

Role-specific additions should remain small. Self-check is first-party verification and never substitutes for Independent Review where that is required.

Self-check is not an issue-generation, minimum-time, target-utilization, pass-count, receipt-generation, or persistence quota. Do not manufacture findings, taxonomy, persistence, repeated work, source collection, polish, Issues, escalation, or receipts merely to demonstrate that this checklist ran.
